The Full Sweep

How the curriculum runs across the 6 weeks

The course opens with a shared Saturday session, splits into Weekday (Mon/Wed/Fri) and Weekend (Sat/Sun) tracks for the four teaching weeks in between, and closes with a second shared Saturday. Session numbers below follow the Weekday Track; the Weekend Track covers the identical curriculum, in the same order, with the same live instructor, across half as many (longer) sessions. All theory comes before any lab, within each module group — the course is built so you reason through a scenario before you ever open a transaction.

1 Opening Session — orientation, what a control is, the SAP landscape, course induction WEEK 1 · SATURDAY · BOTH TRACKS
Guide review, Q&A, and course induction. Opens cold on Book 1 — two permissions sitting next to each other for five years. Covers what a control actually is (the five moving parts, the severity ladder), a survey of the SAP landscape (ECC vs. S/4HANA, SAP Security, GRC, Fiori), Thornfield and its cast, and the Nine-Step Reasoning Chain (taught once, used every session after).
2 Theory — Module 1, Access, Identity & Authorization WEEK 2 · MON 14 SEP
Module 1 at full depth — Books 1, 16 and 17; Books 4, 7 and 13 on what differs.
  • Planning Card 1 — completed in session
3 Theory — Module 2, Change, Operations & the Data Estate WEEK 2 · WED 16 SEP
Runs the full Nine-Step Chain against Book 2, The Emergency Change — transports, the DEV→QAS→PRD path, and the population trap that catches everybody once. Also Book 18, The Untested Backup, and Book 19, The Interface Gap, both at full depth. Extensions: Books 5, 8, 20, 21, 22.
  • Planning Card 2 — completed in session
4 Theory — Module 3, Record to Report, the GRC Platform & Governance WEEK 2 · FRI 18 SEP
The heaviest reading week — Books climbing from one posted journal entry to a CFO's signature. Anchors at full depth: Book 3 (The Approval Limit), Book 14 (The Rule That Never Fired), Book 12 (The Four-Day Clock — ICFR vs. disclosure controls, and the SEC cyber-disclosure clock). Extensions: Books 6, 9, 10, 11, 15. Introduces the workpaper standard.
  • Planning Card 3 — completed in session
5 Lab — Module 1, Access, Identity & Authorization WEEK 3 · MON 21 SEP
First half: the facilitator demonstrates three tests end to end in a live SAP system — Book 1 (Access Risk Analysis, PFCG, SUIM), Book 16 (Firefighter Log Report, SU01), Book 17 (SU01, SUIM). Second half: you run the same three tests yourself, on your own login.
Due before Wed 23 Sep: Workpaper 1 — Book 17, The Leaver
6 Lab — Module 2, Change, Operations & the Data Estate WEEK 3 · WED 23 SEP
Workpaper feedback, then three tests demonstrated and run: Book 2 (STMS import history, SE01), Book 18 (DB13, DB12, SM37), Book 19 (SM58, SMQ1/SMQ2, WE02). Second half, you drive.
Due before Fri 25 Sep: Workpaper 2 — Book 2, The Emergency Change
7 Lab — Module 3, Record to Report, GRC Platform & Governance WEEK 3 · FRI 25 SEP
Workpaper feedback, then Book 3 (FBL3N, FB03, SWI1), Book 14 (GRC Process Control rule view, or the SM37 fallback), Book 12 (SM21, SM20). Second half, you drive.
Due by Fri 2 Oct: Workpaper 3 — Book 3, The Approval Limit
8 Theory — Module 4a, Business Application Controls (money leaving the company) WEEK 4 · MON 28 SEP
Module 4 is a different layer from everything before it — controls built into configuration that operate on every transaction, with no human deciding to perform them. Books 24 and 25 at full depth.
  • Planning Card 4 — completed in session
9 Theory — Module 4b, Business Application Controls (what the system was told) WEEK 4 · WED 30 SEP
Books 26 and 27 at full depth.
  • Planning Card 5 — completed in session
10 Theory — Module 4c, Business Application Controls (what runs unwatched) WEEK 4 · FRI 2 OCT
Books 23 and 28 at full depth.
  • Planning Card 6 — completed in session
11 Lab — Module 4a, Business Application Controls WEEK 5 · MON 5 OCT
A different kind of lab from Modules 1–3 — no reports to run. This one reads configuration tables directly: EKPO, RSEG. Books 24 and 25 demonstrated, then run on your own login.
12 Lab — Module 4b, Business Application Controls WEEK 5 · WED 7 OCT
Continues in configuration tables — CDPOS, OMR6. Books 26 and 27 demonstrated, then run on your own login.
13 Lab — Module 4c, Business Application Controls WEEK 5 · FRI 9 OCT
Books 23 and 28 demonstrated, then run on your own login. Less clicking, more deciding what a single configuration field means.
Due by Fri 16 Oct: Workpaper 4 — Book 24, The Match That Wasn't
14 Closing Session — post-class review, business cycles, programme close WEEK 6 · SAT 17 OCT · BOTH TRACKS
Course wrap-up: how you're assessed, the things you can now do that you couldn't six weeks ago, and what comes next.
