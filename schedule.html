Time zone

All times below are US Eastern Time

Trainees join from the US, the UK, Europe, and Nigeria. Every time on this page is shown in US Eastern Standard Time (EST, UTC−5), with UK Time (GMT, UTC+0) and West Africa Time (WAT, UTC+1) alongside.

Daylight Saving note: ET and UK times below are standard time — EST (UTC−5) and GMT (UTC+0). During US Daylight Saving Time (mid-March to early November), Eastern clocks move to EDT (UTC−4) and run 1 hour later than shown. During UK Summer Time (late March to late October), UK clocks move to BST (UTC+1) and also run 1 hour later than shown. The US and UK switch on slightly different dates, so for a couple of weeks each spring and autumn the gap between them can shift briefly. WAT does not observe daylight saving, so the WAT time never changes.

Course 1 · The Full Sweep

How the curriculum runs across the 4 weeks

The course opens with two live Saturdays — a pre-class introduction, then orientation — runs live Monday/Wednesday/Friday for Theory in Week 1 and the SAP demo lab in Week 2, and closes with a final Saturday wrap. Video Course trainees get two extra live Saturdays (Theory Close/Lab Intro and Lab Close) in place of the live weekday sessions. Here's what each part of that arc covers.

– Pre-class Introduction — a map of what you're about to read SATURDAY 1 · SHARED
A live session that introduces the course — not the reading yet. You leave with the three pre-class guides and a plan to read them properly before Orientation. Both formats join this session live, together.
– Orientation Q&A — what a control is, the SAP landscape, the case SATURDAY 2 · SHARED
Opens cold on Book 1 — two permissions sitting next to each other for five years. A structured Q&A on the three pre-class guides, then covers what a control actually is (the five moving parts, the severity ladder), a survey of the SAP landscape (ECC vs. S/4HANA, SAP Security, GRC, Fiori), Thornfield and its cast, and the Nine-Step Reasoning Chain (taught once, used every session after). Both formats join this session live, together — then the main course and the case begin.
1 Module 1 Theory — Access, Identity & Authorization WEEK 1 · MONDAY
Books 1, 16 and 17 at full depth; Books 4, 7, 13 and 20 on what differs.
  • Planning Card exercise — Book 16 or 17, in breakout rooms
2 Module 2 Theory — Change, Operations & the Data Estate WEEK 1 · WEDNESDAY
Runs the full Nine-Step Chain against Book 2, The Emergency Change — transports, the DEV→QAS→PRD path, and the population trap that catches everybody once. Also Book 18, The Untested Backup, and Book 19, The Interface Gap, both at full depth. Extensions: Books 5, 8, 21, 22. Includes a live app demo of the SAP Testing Execution Guide.
  • Planning Card exercise — Book 5, 8 or 21
3 Module 3 Theory — Record to Report, the GRC Platform & Governance WEEK 1 · FRIDAY
The heaviest reading week — eight Books climbing from one posted journal entry to a CFO's signature. Anchors at full depth: Book 3 (The Approval Limit), Book 14 (The Rule That Never Fired), Book 12 (The Four-Day Clock — ICFR vs. disclosure controls, and the SEC cyber-disclosure clock). Extensions: Books 6, 9, 15, 10, 11. Introduces the workpaper standard.
  • Planning Card exercise — Book 15, 9 or 6
– Theory Close & Lab Intro SATURDAY 3 · VIDEO COURSE ONLY
Live for Video Course trainees only — a Q&A on the three Theory modules, then an introduction to the SAP demo lab ahead of Week 2. Live Class trainees don't need this session; they've already argued the modules live during the week.
4 Module 1 Lab — Access, Identity & Authorization WEEK 2 · MONDAY
First hour: the facilitator demonstrates three tests end to end in a live SAP system — Book 1 (Access Risk Analysis, PFCG, SUIM), Book 16 (Firefighter Log Report, SU01), Book 17 (SU01, SUIM). Second hour: you run the same three tests yourself, on your own login.
Due before the next session: Workpaper Lab — Book 17, The Leaver
5 Module 2 Lab — Change, Operations & the Data Estate WEEK 2 · WEDNESDAY
Workpaper feedback, then three tests demonstrated and run: Book 2 (STMS import history, SE01), Book 18 (DB13, DB12, SM37), Book 19 (SM58, SMQ1/SMQ2, WE02). Second hour, you drive.
Due before the next session: Workpaper Lab — Book 2, The Emergency Change
6 Module 3 Lab — R2R, GRC Platform & Governance WEEK 2 · FRIDAY
Workpaper feedback, then Book 3 (FBL3N, FB03, SWI1), Book 14 (GRC Process Control rule view, or the SM37 fallback), Book 12 (SM21, SM20). Second hour, you drive.
Due within one week: Workpaper Lab — Book 3, The Approval Limit
– Lab Close & Workpaper Clinic SATURDAY 4 · VIDEO COURSE ONLY
Live for Video Course trainees only — Q&A on the lab, a clinic on the workpapers submitted so far, and their live-weekday-equivalent close. Live Class trainees don't need this session; they've already driven the lab live during the week.
– Post-class Q&A / Wrap — assessment review, what you can now do, what's next SATURDAY 5 · SHARED
Read beforehand: the 8 post-class chapters (S/4HANA landscape, ITGC and Basis, and the four core business cycles), self-study, no sessions. Then the whole cohort is back in one room for the last time — how you're assessed, the things you can now do that you couldn't four weeks ago, and what comes next. Both formats join this session live, together.
