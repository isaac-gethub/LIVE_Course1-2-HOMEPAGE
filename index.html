The promise

What four weeks will and won't do for you

Say this up front, because anybody promising otherwise is selling something.

This course will not

  • Make you an SAP expert in four weeks
  • Teach you ninety transaction codes by rote
  • Replace hands-on time on a real engagement
  • Hand you a certificate for showing up

This course will

  • Get you stating the risk, the control objective, the population and the evidence plan — before you name a screen
  • Show you exactly what a reviewer will push back on, and why
  • Give you a method that survives every SAP system you'll ever work in
  • Put you on your own login on a live S/4HANA server, testing a real deficiency, with a facilitator watching you reason

How it runs

Read it. Argue it. Run it in SAP.

Three layers, in order, every time. Skip the first and the second becomes somebody reading to you.

01
SAP from zero
A live three-hour session that introduces the course — not the reading. You leave with three prerequisite guides and an eight-day plan to read them properly.
02
Orientation & your questions
A structured Q&A on the reading — your questions, answered by the group, in conversation — then the main course and the case begin.
03
Theory — three sessions
We reason through Module 1, 2 and 3 on paper first. Every session ends on a decision you have to bring an answer to, before you've touched SAP once.
04
The SAP demo lab
First hour, the facilitator demonstrates three tests end to end on a real system. Second hour, you run the same three tests yourself.
Display access only — the habit you'll need on a live client system
05
Post-class reading
No sessions. Eight chapters — the S/4HANA landscape, ITGC and Basis, and the four core business cycles — read at your own pace.
06
Final Q&A and close
The whole cohort back in one room. What you can now do, what comes next, and — if you've earned it — a word about you, specifically.
